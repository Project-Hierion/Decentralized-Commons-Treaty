# Registry of Signatories

This is the public registry of individuals, projects, and organizations that have ratified the Decentralized Commons Treaty.

---

## How to Join

1. Add your name/project to this list.
2. Submit a Pull Request.
3. Your ratification is recorded upon merge.

**No permission required. Just show up.**

## How to Verify

- **GitHub PR:** Link to your ratification PR.
- **PGP Signature:** Include your signed commit or separate signature file.
- **Public Statement:** Link to a blog post, tweet, or public declaration.

---

## Revocation

If you wish to revoke your ratification, submit a PR removing your name. Please include a brief explanation.
