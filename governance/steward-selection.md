# Steward Selection

---

## Role of the Steward

The Steward coordinates the commons. Duties include:

- Convening meetings and summits
- Maintaining the charter and standards documents
- Facilitating dispute resolution
- Reporting transparently on all activities
- Maintaining the public registry of signatories

---

## Restrictions

- No financial stake in any protocol, platform, or token
- No favoring one project over another
- Fixed term (2 years), renewable by community vote

---

## Selection Process

### 1. Nomination
- Any active participant may nominate a candidate.
- Self-nomination is permitted.
- Nominations must include a brief statement of intent.

### 2. Review Period
- 30-day public comment period on all nominees.
- Candidates may be questioned by the community.

### 3. Vote
- Active participants vote.
- One person, one vote.
- Simple majority wins.

### 4. Transition
- New Steward takes office immediately upon confirmation.
- Outgoing Steward provides a handover report.

---

## Removal

The Steward may be removed by:

- **75% supermajority vote** of active participants
- **Resignation**

Grounds for removal:
- Bias or misconduct
- Failure to perform duties
- Violation of the Treaty
