# Dispute Resolution

---

## Overview

Disputes are resolved by the community, not by courts. The goal is consensus, not victory.

---

## Types of Disputes

### Technical Disputes
- Two implementations disagree on a standard.
- The community convenes a technical working group.
- The working group's recommendation is published for comment.
- If consensus cannot be reached, both implementations may coexist until convergence.

### Governance Disputes
- A Steward is accused of bias or misconduct.
- The community investigates.
- If substantiated, the Steward is removed.

### Violations of the Treaty
- A signatory violates the Treaty.
- They are publicly identified.
- Other signatories may cease interoperability.
- The violator may regain standing by correcting the violation.

---

## Principles

- **Good Faith** — All parties act in good faith.
- **Transparency** — All proceedings are public.
- **Consensus** — Resolution is by rough consensus, not force.

---

## Escalation

If a dispute cannot be resolved:

1. The Steward facilitates mediation.
2. If mediation fails, the community votes.
3. If the community is deadlocked, the dispute is tabled for 6 months and revisited.
