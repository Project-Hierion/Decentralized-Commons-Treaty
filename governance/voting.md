# Voting Procedures

---

## Overview

Voting is used for:

- Steward selection and removal
- Treaty amendments
- Dispute resolution
- Any other matter requiring a formal decision

---

## Principles

- **One Person, One Voice** — No token-weighting. No corporate veto.
- **Rough Consensus** — Not unanimity. Sustained opposition blocks; mere disagreement does not.
- **Transparency** — All votes are public.

---

## Who Can Vote

Active participants are those who have:

1. Ratified the Treaty (listed in signatories/registry.md)
2. Participated in at least one community discussion or meeting in the past 12 months
3. Not had their standing revoked

---

## Quorum

- No formal quorum required.
- Decisions are made by those who show up.
- Substantive decisions (amendments, Steward removal) require active discussion, not just votes.

---

## Voting Period

- **Steward selection:** 14 days
- **Steward removal:** 21 days
- **Amendments:** 30 days (after 6-month comment period)
- **Disputes:** 14 days

---

## Recording

All votes are recorded in the repository.

- GitHub PR votes = comments + approvals
- Public statements = linked to the vote
- Meeting votes = recorded in meeting notes
